## Singapore — MUIS (Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura)
URL: https://www.muis.gov.sg/resources/media-releases/muis-removes-compounded-fidyah-requirement-and-announces-zakat-fitrah-rates-for-ramadan-2026-1447h/
Fetched OK (curl). Date on page: 16 February 2026.
Quotes:
- "Fidyah rates updated to S$4.00 per day based on actual meal costs and Zakat Fitrah set at S$5.00-S$8.00 per person."
- "Based on the new calculation methodology, the new fidyah rate is S$4.00 per day. ... the previous fidyah rate of S$1.40 was established in 2014."
- "The kafarah rate has also been streamlined to align with fidyah at S$4.00 per person."
- "The new approach moves away from the weight of rice-based system."
- "For Ramadan 2026, Zakat Fitrah rates remain accessible. The normal rate is maintained at $5.00 per person. While the higher rate is set at $8.00 per person - a $0.10 increase from last year. These rates are based on the median price of normal and higher-grade rice"
- Transition: "Members of the public who have prepared to pay fidyah at the previous rate of S$1.40 may do so during the transition period ... new rate of S$4.00 will be fully implemented from 1 Syawal 1447H onwards"
- Also: MUIS removed the default compounded fidyah for delayed qada.

## SA' (صاع) WEIGHT CONVENTIONS — summary of what bodies state
 - Egypt Dar al-Ifta: 2.04 kg wheat ("يعادل (2.04) كيلو جرامًا من القمح")
 - Jordan Iftaa: 2.5 kg wheat ("الصاع يساوي (2.5 كغم) تقريباً")
 - Morocco Higher Council of Ulema: ≈2.5 kg grain or flour ("كيلوغرامين ونصف تقريبا (2.5 كيلوغرام)")
 - UAE Fatwa Council: 2.5 kg rice ; fidya per poor person per day 3.250 kg wheat (البر)
 - Indonesia BAZNAS: 2.5 kg or 3.5 litres rice/staple
 - Kuwait Zakat House Sharia board: ≈2.5 kg rice (undated page)
 - Qatar Awqaf: 2.5 kg rice (stated in earlier years' notices; not restated in the 2026 article read)
 - Malaysia: 1 gantang Baghdad = 2.6 kg rice (Selangor LZS, Johor); Negeri Sembilan 1 gantang = 2.751 kg; fidyah = 1/4 gantang (secupak) i.e. fitrah ÷ 4 (Sarawak/Kedah notes on JAWHAR)
 - Brunei: 2 kg 268 g rice
 - Pakistan CII: wheat = half sa' ≈ 2 kg; barley/dates/munaqqa = 1 sa' ≈ 4 kg ("as a precaution")
 - Algeria Ministry: sa' = 2 kg (2025 statement)
 - Saudi Arabia: ≈3 kg (Saudipedia; Ibn Baz fatwa)
 - USA FCNA: sa' in pounds per item (rice 5.40 lb, wheat flour 4.00 lb, dates 4.63 lb, barley 3.53 lb, oats 2.43 lb, pasta 2.20 lb)
 - Singapore MUIS: fitrah based on median rice price (weight not stated in release); fidyah/kafarah now meal-cost based, no longer rice-weight based.
